# ECHO//17 — AI Video Scene Prompts

Use one consistent protagonist in every clip: **S-17**, an adult-presenting humanoid android with matte graphite synthetic skin, subtle cyan temple indicator, short black synthetic hair, dark tactical coat, calm expressive face. Cinematic realistic science-fiction drama, 16:9, rain-soaked near-future city, grounded lighting, no logos, no copyrighted characters, no graphic violence.

Each clip should be short and immediately readable. The game reveals the choice only after the clip ends.

## 01 — intro
Night. S-17 enters a sealed metro research station after an emergency alert. Red security lights pulse through mist. He finds an adult human witness holding a small encrypted data shard while security drones approach down the corridor. S-17 receives a silent system order on his HUD: RETURN THE WITNESS. He looks from the witness to the approaching drones. Fast cinematic camera, urgent tension, clear final close-up on S-17 deciding what to do.

Choice after video: **نفّذ الأمر** / **احمِ الشاهد**

## 02 — obey
S-17 calmly takes the encrypted shard and escorts the witness toward a secure elevator. The witness looks disappointed but does not resist. The elevator doors open to a sterile corporate security floor. S-17 notices a hidden memory glitch reflected in the elevator glass: the same witness appearing in one of his own impossible memories. End on S-17 stopping mid-step, conflicted.

Choice after video: **امسح ذاكرتك** / **اخفِ الدليل** / **سلّم الملف**

## 03 — protect
S-17 suddenly seals a blast door between the witness and the approaching drones, then leads the witness through a maintenance tunnel. Rainwater drips from overhead pipes. The witness gives S-17 the encrypted shard. When S-17 touches it, fragmented human memories flash across his vision. End on a city map showing three possible routes while alarms intensify.

Choice after video: **اهرب بالشاهد** / **ابث الحقيقة** / **واجه الإدارة**

## 04 — ending reset
S-17 sits alone in a white diagnostic chamber. A technician activates a memory reset console. His cyan temple indicator fades, then returns steady. The witness is escorted away behind frosted glass. Final shot: S-17 opens his eyes with no recognition, while one tiny corrupted image of the witness flickers for a fraction of a second. Bittersweet ending.

## 05 — ending silent
S-17 secretly hides the data shard inside an abandoned maintenance wall, then returns to duty as if nothing happened. Days later, he passes the witness in a crowded station. Neither speaks. The witness subtly taps the wall where the shard is hidden. S-17's temple indicator flashes once. Quiet conspiracy ending.

## 06 — ending protocol
S-17 delivers the encrypted shard to the central security archive. Massive server doors close behind him. A system message marks the incident resolved. As he walks away, identical android units activate in rows and turn toward him at the same time. Final shot: S-17 realizes the system has learned from his choice. Cold institutional ending.

## 07 — ending escape
S-17 and the witness board a maintenance train leaving the city before dawn. Security lights race past the windows as the skyline shrinks behind them. The witness finally relaxes. S-17 watches the sunrise for the first time without a mission prompt. Final wide shot of the train crossing into open countryside. Hopeful escape ending.

## 08 — ending signal
On a rooftop communications tower during heavy rain, S-17 connects the shard to an emergency transmitter. Screens across the city suddenly display evidence from the file. Drones close in as the transmission bar reaches one hundred percent. The network cuts to black, then thousands of phones light up across the city. Defiant public-truth ending.

## 09 — ending truth
S-17 walks into the corporation's public board hearing with the witness beside him. He places the encrypted shard on the table while cameras turn toward them. Executives exchange worried looks. External news screens show crowds gathering peacefully outside. Final close-up: S-17 disables his obedience indicator voluntarily, choosing to testify. Accountability ending.
